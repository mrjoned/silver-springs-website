Hey everyone - big weekend ahead! Aside from the PvP events, there's new charms, the Lucky Rush, and general updates with an upcoming build contest FYI.

📿Charms

There are 12 new charms to find:

  • 💎The Valor Shop has 5 new charms. They are listed in-game in ‘/valor charms’, but a few examples:

    • 🗿Bobblehead: Gain the Luck effect permanently while wearing your own head.

    • 💧Prince Rupert's Drop: Your hazard enchants activate with massive power when struck from behind.

    • 🗝️Spare Key: Your first charm slot can be modified even while your charms case is locked.

  • 💀The Crypt Trial Shop sells the Metal Detector, which extracts scrap in place of junk when brushing suspicious sediments.

  • 🎈The Boardwalk sells Balloon Animal, which causes allied mobs to pop on death, applying a radial stun and damage. Tragic!

  • There are 5 other charms findable either around spawn or via gameplay, including the Sanguine Phial. Keep an eye out!

Some old charms have also been updated:

  • 🍵Coffee Beans: Reworked! Gain speed, haste, and double experience during sunrise.

  • 🏌️Golf Club: Reworked! Your attacks gain range, ignore some mob knockback resist, and deal double knockback to small targets.

  • 🔻Nerfs: Reduced Adventurer's Tricorne boss payout to 250 silver. Made Hourglass Sands only work on utility items. Reduced Runic Compass proc rate.

The rush starts in just a few! In addition to the Core Bonuses, this rush has two main benefits:

  • +3 Luck all weekend. Simple but effective - and good for hunting for charms!,

  • Lucky Events can randomly activate to amplify certain events. Keep an eye out!,
