🍄‍🟫Spooky Springs Voting

  • Now that the build phase is complete, you can go vote for your top 3 Spooky Springs plots.

  • To vote, stand in a plot and use `/halloween vote <1st, 2nd, or 3rd>`. Tab completion is available.

  • You can change your votes freely by re-casting them, and you don't need to vote for all 3 placements. You can't vote for your own plot.

  • Thanks to all who have entered!

📝General Updates

  • 🖼️Paintings can once again be changed in a stonecutter.

  • 👮Copper bars and chains can once again be scraped with an axe to unwax or deoxidize them.
