✨Paragons

Starmarsh™ and ElectricSparx have officially received their Paragons, which I'm formally announcing here in an effort for the Paragons to be more well-known. These are unique items designed in partnership with each of them, though as usual with Paragons there's a possibility that a subdued version of one or both of these becomes generally available in the future (that's where the Runic Tome came from!)

  • Following a spark of inspiration, @Starmarsh™ has engineered CoSMOS, a marvelous rebreather which can alchemically empower the wearer, mutating their physiology or injecting potions from provided reagents.,

  • From deep within the Arcanum's now-corrupted lower shelves, @ElectricSparx has discovered the Eldritch Codex, a Runic Grimoire whose arcane script has been altered by the void, now casting an entirely alternate set of corrupted spells.

  • We'll soon add a ‘/paragon’ command to view the existing Paragons in-game, for those curious.,
