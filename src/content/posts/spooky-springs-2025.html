Spooky Springs is a communal build contest run during the Halloween season:

📖Overview:

  • The event takes place in a gloomy mushroom cave at `/warp SpookySprings`.

  • The cave contains 20 plots. Approach one and right click the sign on its front wall to claim it for free. You can only claim one plot.

  • Plots are 17x61x17, extending 30 blocks up and down. Your entire contest entry must be contained within this space.

  • If you want to build with a friend, stand in your plot and use `/as addfriend <name>` to give them permission to build there.

  • You all then help decide the winners! After the build period, you'll have 2 days to vote for your top 3 plots. You can't vote for your own.

    • Details on how to vote will be sent once the build phase ends.

📝Details:

  • 🎃Theme: Halloween. That's it - anything seasonal, spooky, or scary is valid!

  • 🗓️Due Date: Wednesday, October 29, 2025 11:59 PM PDT (Also listed on the calendar)

  • ✏️Standard Info: Can be found here on discord or here on the website.

  • 🏆Special Prize: Entrants for the top 3 submissions earn +1 Charms slot and a ton of Halloween candy.
