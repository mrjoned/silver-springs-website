Hey everyone. Been a bit, but we've got a sizeable update including a new game version, our Halloween content for the year, and some general updates!

🤖The Copper Age

❗ We've updated to 1.21.10, The Copper Age game drop, which includes Copper Golems who can sort your loot, copper decor, armor, and tools, and shelf blocks. On SSS, Copper Golems have unique head drops for each oxidation stage and can sort loot into barrels. Copper is not yet usable with Impetus of Hephaestus. Enjoy!

Spooky Springs is back! This year, you'll choose from plots within a large, gloomy mushroom cave. We'll add more plots if demand requires. For newcomers, we've got a guide to Spooky Springs, which is where most details (including prizes) can be found. Link below!

🌕The Blood Moon Rises

  • 🌕When a full moon rises in the survival or resource world, a Blood Moon activates - a sleepless night of dangerous foes.

  • 🩸Stronger enemies appear during Blood Moons, but they drop better loot, including bonus XP and occult items. See here on Discord or here on the website for full details!

  • 🍬Until the end of October, more witches, black cats, bats, and more will spawn naturally. Seasonal enemies may drop special candy food items.

  • 🗓️We have ideas to make non-automatic Blood Moons a permanent feature if there is interest. Let us know what you think!

📝General Updates

  • ✂️Dispensers now properly activate runecrafted enchantments of shears and flint and steel, and work with infinite buckets.

  • 🍼The Potion Launcher now works with the Impetus of Dionysus happy hour potion discount.

  • ⛏️ Magnetism now leaves behind uncobbled blocks if used with Silk Touch.

  • 📦Avatar of Shulkers once again properly fires its shulker bullet.
