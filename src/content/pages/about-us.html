Our community

Get to know the community

🌸Silver Springs Survival

Silver Springs Survival is dedicated to an enjoyable, customized vanilla experience. Over the years, we’ve put countless hours into fine-tuning our world while holding true to the Minecraft gameplay we love.

We’re fairly small, but our community is tight-knit, warm, and active. Some staff and players have been with us since the very beginning—and some were around even before that.

Whether you’re here to build, explore, take on a challenge, or spend time with friends, there’s room to find your own way to play.

How we play

Common sense rules

We keep moderation plugins minimal and expect players to treat one another with respect

01 / YOUR OWN WAY

Build alone or with friends
