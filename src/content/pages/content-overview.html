Apply the enchantment

  1. Start with the enchanted book

    Hold the enchanted book you want to apply and right-click the Arcanum.

  2. Submit your equipment

    Switch to the item you want to enchant, then right-click the Arcanum with that item.

Repeat this process for each enchantment you want to apply.

Upgrade one level at a time

Apply another Level I book of the same enchantment to increase the item’s existing enchantment by one level. Repeat the book-then-item process at the Arcanum for each upgrade.

LEVEL I

First Level I book

An item without that enchantment gains Level I.

LEVEL II

Second Level I book

An item with Level I upgrades to Level II.

LEVEL III

Third Level I book

An item with Level II upgrades to Level III.

This example uses an enchantment that supports Level III. Check the Hub for the maximum level of your chosen enchantment.
RECIPE COSTS

Runecrafted costs

Starting without the enchantment, Level III needs three Level I books: three complete recipes, or 15 runes in total.

SPECIAL CASES

Fixed Vanilla+ upgrades

A named, fixed-level upgrade uses its recipe once. Use the Hub’s displayed cost for these exceptions.

Mythical, Curse, and Vanilla entries have no rune cost because they’re found in other ways!

Cast spells with the Runic Tome

A spellbook that turns your runes into Runic Blasts. Find it in the Cultist Raid section of the Trial Shop for 250 Cultist Raid Trial Points.

  1. Open the Tome

    Hold the Runic Tome and sneak-right-click to open its five-slot rune inventory.

  2. Choose a rune

    Insert runes into the inventory, then right-click an inserted rune to select it.

  3. Cast a Runic Blast

    Close the inventory and right-click with the Tome. Casting spends XP, and the selected rune determines the spell.

Runic Surges · earn Boundary Shards

  1. Bring Bad Omen

    Have Bad Omen active before entering an uncorrupted Rune Locale. Use /warp RuneHub and the appropriate portal to reach the Locale.

  2. Trigger the Surge

    Entering consumes Bad Omen and starts the event. Its starting level determines the Surge’s difficulty tier.

  3. Defeat the apex

    Take part in the Surge and defeat its apex. When the apex is defeated, all participants receive runes and 1–5 Boundary Shards, scaling with the tier.

DIFFICULTY

Five difficulty tiers

Regular Surges range from Tier I to Tier V. Higher tiers are more dangerous.

EXPLORATION

Multiple active Locales

Different Locales can have Surges running at the same time.

Ominous Rush bonus: Tier III+ Surges can also award Valor Badges during an Ominous Rush.

Remove an enchantment

  1. Start with Boundary Shards

    Hold Boundary Shards and right-click the Arcanum.

  2. Submit the item

    Hold the item you want to remove an enchantment from and right-click the Arcanum. A selection GUI opens.

  3. Choose the enchantment

    Click the enchantment you want to remove from the GUI’s list. With enough Shards, the selected enchantment is removed completely. Otherwise, the Arcanum tells you that you do not have enough Shards.

Whole enchantments only. You cannot remove one level at a time. A Level III enchantment costs three Boundary Shards to remove in full; it cannot be reduced to Level II for one Shard.

Troubleshooting at the Arcanum

What happens if enchantments conflict?

The newly applied enchantment overrides the older conflicting enchantment. Check your item before applying a replacement.

What happens if I enter the wrong recipe?

Failed recipes produce a Curse. The submitted runes are consumed to make the Curse and are not returned. Follow the listed rune order carefully.

What happens with an invalid application?

Invalid enchantment applications return an error in chat explaining what went wrong.

Enchantment types & recipes

Do Mythical enchantments use rune recipes?

They use a different acquisition route. Check the associated item or reward in the Valor Shop guide.

Where do recipes and descriptions live?

The Hub holds the enchantment reference and planning tools. This guide explains the process around them.

Catalog & shopping planner

How to use the shopping planner

  1. Find your next purchase

    Search by name or description. Use the category and shop filters to narrow the catalog.

  2. Add the quantities you need

    Add items to the shopping list. Armor quantities count pieces, so choose the number of pieces you intend to buy.

  3. Collect your requirements

    Read the merchant subtotals and combined requirements. Different Trials’ points remain separate currencies.

  4. Make your purchases

    For Valor Shop and Trial Shop items, find the item and right-click the appropriate sign. For artifacts, trade with the villager underneath the lower-level staircase in the Valor Shop. The list also groups Boardwalk and village-vendor purchases separately.

  5. Check off your purchases

    Mark completed purchases in the shopping list. Copy the list when you want a text version.

Checked-off purchases remain included in totals. The list records your plan; checking an item does not buy it in-game.

Two kinds of gem

FIVE TYPES

Stat gems

Agility · Haste · Tenacity · Vigor · Favor. Each uses one stat socket.

TWO TYPES

Armor gems

Preservation · Fortitude. These do not consume stat sockets. Armor cannot be improved beyond diamond quality.

Diamond quality is the cap. Some Valor Armor sets use dyeable leather for their appearance. Armor gems let that lower-tier armor reach diamond-quality armor values while keeping its appearance; they cannot improve it beyond diamond quality.

THE DETAILS

Socket a gem at a smithing table

  1. Open a smithing table

    Bring your armor and gem. The same process works for player armor, horse armor, wolf armor, and harnesses.

  2. Place your armor

    Put the armor in the item slot.

  3. Place your gem

    Put the gem in the material slot. Leave the smithing template slot empty; no template is required.

  4. Check the output

    If all stat sockets are full, adding another stat gem produces no finished item. You cannot add more stat gems beyond the socket limit.

The smithing-table process is the same for all armor types. Harnesses allow six stat sockets; all other armor types allow four.
THE DETAILS

Unsocket Armor

The Valor Shop service costs one Valor Badge and removes all socketed gems from an armor piece. Removed gems are destroyed and are not returned.

Plan before you socket. Unsocketing sacrifices the gems already in that armor piece.

THE DETAILS

Register at the Infinity Altar

  1. Find the portal in the Valor Shop

    When the portal is open, enter it to reach the Infinity Altar.

  2. Choose a pedestal

    Three pedestals await your heirlooms. Hold the item you want to carry forward.

  3. Register your item

    Right-click the pedestal while holding the item to set it aside for the next map.

  4. Keep using your item

    You receive your item back after setting it aside, so you can continue using it for the remainder of the map.

Registration saves the item as it is at that time. Later changes are not carried over automatically. Ask Staff for assistance re-registering an item if you change it.

Keep baby mobs small

Use a golden dandelion to prevent growth. The August 2026 update replaces the old poisonous-potato method.

Permanent mob effects · invulnerability & invisibility

  1. Choose an effect

    Apply Strength to the mob for permanent invulnerability, or Invisibility for permanent invisibility.

  2. Feed a golden apple

    While that effect is active on the mob, feed it a golden apple to apply the corresponding permanent effect. Bring the listed Valor Badges when applying invulnerability.

Bring one egg and five diamonds. Throw the egg at the mob, or punch the mob with the egg, to capture it. Captured mobs retain details such as age, color, and tame status.

Eligible mobs: friendly mobs excluding villagers; Happy Ghasts; Camel Husks; all three Nautilus variants; and any pacified mob, including Shrink Ray victims.

Combat mechanics

Shield parrying

Block within 0.3 seconds before a strike to parry. Melee parries stun; projectile parries reflect.

Shield bashing

Quickly double-block to push nearby enemies in front of you. Axe hits disable shields for 1.6 seconds.

Projectiles & stuns

Projectiles bypass invulnerability frames. Stuns stop actions but break on direct damage. Each Gem of Tenacity also grants +5% stun resistance, reducing stun duration. Explore gem socketing →

Tridents

Craft with three Twinkling Sea Crystals, one Nautilus Shell, and one Prismarine Shard. Riptide conflicts only with Loyalty; Loyalty returns faster and recovers tridents from the void.

Combat skill experience

Splash or lingering potions applied to others grant mcMMO Alchemy experience. The June 26, 2025 update also increased experience gains for the mcMMO archery, mace, and trident skills.

PvP arena exceptions

In PvP arenas, Film Reel does not reduce spell cooldowns. The Aegis Runic Tome spell lasts 5 seconds with a 10-second cooldown. These restrictions apply specifically to PvP arenas.

Use /toggle sounds combat_end to toggle a subtle sound when you leave combat.

Seasonal & community activities

These activities return when announced. Check the calendar and current event post for availability, deadlines, and prizes.

Blood Moons · Halloween season

During the Halloween event, a full moon in a survival or resource world can bring a Blood Moon. Sleeping cannot skip the night. Empowered enemies are bigger, stronger, and faster, but reward more XP and can drop occult items or, rarely, Sanguine Phial. The charm gains stacks at double rate from Blood Moon kills.

Spooky Springs · community build event

Help build a themed Halloween area in designated plots. The location, theme, entry process, voting, and rewards follow that year’s announcement. Watch for the build period and the later voting period.

12 Days of Christmas · fireplace gifts

When the event opens, use /christmas for instructions. Build a fireplace with a fire block and at least two adjacent solid blocks, then register it with /christmas fireplace. Your first daily login brings a present near the fireplace, up to 12 presents. Everyone receives the same gifts in the same order. Check that year’s announcement for the gift window and daily reset timezone.

Build contests · themed survival builds

Follow the announced theme and deadline. Regular contest entries can be built in survival worlds, and you can enter with friends. Submit the coordinates and all contributors to Mr_Simba on Discord or through in-game mail. Existing builds can qualify if they fit the theme. Special events such as Spooky Springs may use their own plot and submission rules.
