Owner & Developer

Mr_Simba

Mr_Simba is the owner and main developer of Silver Springs Survival, otherwise known as Simba. He is very friendly and kind to all players. 

Simba made and maintains almost all of the special plugins that are used on the server.

It is widely believed that Squid Jesus was Simba's first spouse.

Time on the team

Simba first became a Moderator March 9th of 2014, then became Developer on April 27 the same year, and has been the Owner since January 15th of 2015.

Head of Staff

Beccc

Beccc is the Head of Staff (HoS) of Silver Springs Survival, otherwise known as Becc. As the HoS, she works with Mr_Simba on new and upcoming features for the server, as well as making sure the staff are able to do their job as intended.

Time on the team

Becc became a Moderator on the 5th of July 2015, then became an Admin on the 18th of March 2016. On January 29, 2019, Becc was promoted to the rank of HoS.

Head of Fun

MrJoned

MrJoned is the Head of Fun (HoF/Head of Fmemes) of Silver Springs Survival. Otherwise known as Jon, he also goes by James, or the Penguin man. He prefers to lick players he likes as opposed to being a normal person. He also records events, and fun things going on, on the server for his Youtube channel.

He is the worst mod. (Follow him on Twitter!) Despite being the worst mod, MrJoned also has a Twitch and a Youtube you should also follow!

Time on the team

MrJoned first became Moderator March 2nd of 2015 (along with EvilDrac) and has been an admin since September 25th of the same year. On September 17, 2023, Jon became the new Head of Fun.

Designer

Autumn_Bun

Autumn Bun is the Designer of Silver Springs Survival. She is very kind and makes everyone laugh. She is good friends with the owner, Mr_Simba. She primarily designs the custom content on the server such as the Runecrafting system and the Gemcrafting system!

Time on the team

Autumn Bun first became Moderator on October 12th of 2014 (along with Frosty) and has been Admin since November 2014. She then stepped up under the Designer role on September 20th, 2023 in recognition of designing, drafting, and testing most of the custom content on our server.
