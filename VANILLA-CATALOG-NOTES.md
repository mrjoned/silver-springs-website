# Vanilla catalog
Baseline: Java Edition 1.21.11 (the server’s Mounts of Mayhem content), including Lunge.
Levels, supported equipment and vanilla exclusivity were checked against the extracted game data:
https://github.com/misode/mcmeta/tree/1.21.11-data-json/data/minecraft/enchantment
https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21-11

Existing Vanilla records become Enhanced Vanilla without changing their IDs, names, descriptions or server rules.
Existing curses remain Curse. Rune-crafted upgrades retain Runecrafted and their rune recipes.
No duplicate base records are added for the nine enhanced enchantments or the two existing curses.
Channeling deliberately permits Riptide to preserve the server’s documented enhancement.
New entries are normal Pages CMS enchantment records and can be edited there.
New vanilla equipment lists cover the Hub's existing equipment types; this does not add new equipment categories.
